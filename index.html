DAY 01 · NOV 2026

Conveyor motor start/stop

Seal-in, fail-safe stop, E-stop and a latched overload fault

10/10 TESTS PASSED

The problem

An operator presses Start and the conveyor must keep running after the button is released. It must stop on Stop, E-stop or a motor overload, and it must never restart on its own.

What I built · 8 tags, 3 rungs

  • R0Overload memory. Latches the fault when the overload trips; clears only on Reset once the overload is healthy again.
  • R1Motor. Start in parallel with a Motor_Run seal-in, in series with Stop, E-stop OK, overload OK and no fault.
  • R2Fault lamp. Follows the latched fault.

What the results mean

  • Stop is wired normally closed, so a broken wire stops the machine instead of making it impossible to stop.
  • No automatic restart after an E-stop protects the person who just reached into the machine.
  • The latched fault forces someone to find out why the motor tripped before running it again.
